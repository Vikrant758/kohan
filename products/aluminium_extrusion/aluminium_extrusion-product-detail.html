Products

Aluminium Extrusion

Construction & Infrastructure

Aluminium extrusions for buildings, facades and structural frameworks. Lightweight, corrosion-resistant and built to last.

Applications:

  • Window and door frames
  • Curtain wall and facade systems
  • Partitions and false-ceiling sections
  • Railings, handrails and staircase profiles
  • Scaffolding and structural framing

Electrical & Switchgear

Precision profiles for panels, enclosures and busbar systems, combining strength with good electrical and thermal conductivity.

Applications:

  • Switchgear and control panel frames
  • Busbar and conductor profiles
  • Distribution board enclosures
  • Cable trays and trunking
  • Modular panel framing with T-slot profiles

Electronics & Telecommunications

Extruded housings and heat sinks that protect sensitive electronics and dissipate heat efficiently.

Applications:

  • Electronic enclosures and instrument cases
  • Heat sinks for power electronics and LEDs
  • Telecom equipment racks and cabinets
  • Antenna mounts and brackets
  • LED light profiles

Renewable Energy & Solar

Lightweight, weather-resistant profiles for solar mounting systems and renewable energy installations.

Applications:

  • Ground-mounted solar structures
  • Rooftop solar mounting rails
  • Solar panel frames
  • Clamps, rails and purlins
  • Inverter and battery enclosures

Rolling Stock

High-strength extrusions for railway coaches and metro rolling stock, reducing weight while meeting structural demands.

Applications:

  • Coach body and roof profiles
  • Window frames and interior panels
  • Luggage racks and seating frames
  • Floor and side-wall sections
  • Door and partition systems

Transportation & Mobility

Extrusions for automotive, bus and EV applications, helping reduce vehicle weight and improve efficiency.

Applications:

  • Vehicle body and chassis components
  • Crash management and bumper beams
  • EV battery enclosures and trays
  • Bus and truck body profiles
  • Roof rails and trim

OEM & Engineering Industries

Standard and custom profiles, along with fitting accessories, for machine builders and engineering OEMs.

Applications:

  • T-slot profiles for machine frames and guards
  • Conveyor and automation structures
  • Workstations and assembly tables
  • Brackets, connectors and fasteners for profile systems
  • Custom profiles as per client drawings
